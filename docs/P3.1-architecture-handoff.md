## 3. Article XVI and read-only enforcement

Constitution article count is **16**. Article XVI: a `bin/lab-verify*` MUST NOT create, modify, move, chmod, or delete anything under the evidence root. If a gate needs a workspace, it copies into `logs/review/<id>/scratch/`.

`bin/lab-gate-readonly-check <gate>` builds a recursive evidence manifest, runs the gate, rebuilds, and asserts equality. Mismatch names the first changed path and exits non-zero.
