# ADR-P6.1-1 — Treat the P6 null as an instrument defect until diagnosed

Status: accepted at P6.1 J001; indexed at P8 close-out.

The original all-refusal result could not be reported as model inability until primary completions ruled out the
instrument. J001 established rigid locator grammar and vocabulary mismatch as proximate causes, so the result was an
instrument defect. The Article XV Arm B amendment corrected framing and extraction without weakening refusal or
exposing the answer. This file indexes the decision of record in `logs/review/J001/adr.md` and the P6.1 plan; it does
not change the frozen amendment.
