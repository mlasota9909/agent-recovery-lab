## Objective

Build `bin/lab-verify-p1`, run every acceptance criterion with its tier, sweep for idempotence, hash-freeze the two new
contracts, verify P0's three contracts are still untouched, and write the acceptance memo that opens P2.

## Steps

1. `bin/lab-verify-p1 [--json]` runs AC-P1-01 through AC-P1-24 and prints, per criterion: id, **tier**, command,
   expected, observed, PASS/FAIL. It prints **separate per-tier totals** in the mandated form:

   ```
   TIER M: <p>/<n> · TIER A: <p>/<n> · TOTAL: <p>/<n>
   ```

   A single undifferentiated total is prohibited (Article XIII).
2. **No prose matching anywhere.** Every criterion asserts on live state or a machine-readable field. If you find
   yourself searching a markdown file for a token, you have violated Article XIII — the exact defect P0.1 was raised to
   repair. There is one tier A criterion (AC-P1-22) and it asserts on JSON fields.
3. **Idempotence sweep (AC-P1-23).** Invoke every P1 CLI a second time with identical arguments and assert each reports
   `"changed": false` and mutates nothing. Verify by hashing the artefact set before and after the sweep.
4. **Contract freeze.** Record SHA-256 of `contracts/results-row.md`, `contracts/ledger-integrity.md` and
   `etc/results-row.schema.json`, and compare against `logs/review/W002/contract-hashes.json`. Any drift during the
   tranche is a failure, not a note.
5. **P0 contracts unchanged (AC-P1-24).** Re-verify the three P0 contracts against
   `logs/review/R007/contracts-unchanged.md`.
6. Also confirm `bin/lab-verify` (P0's runner) still reports 18/18 with tiers, so P1 has not regressed the substrate.
7. Write `docs/p1-acceptance-memo.md` containing:
   - the tiered acceptance result;
   - the measured cycle times and the sample-size plan headline;
   - **the limitation statement**: restore and validation are stubbed in P1, so no claim about recovery correctness is
     yet supported; that is P4's loop-closing test;
   - the honest note that the oracle is SC-01-specific and that SC-03/SC-04 need a different optimum definition;
   - a P2 go/no-go recommendation, and the ledger schema P2 must not deviate from.
8. Update `sdd/specs/P1-ground-truth-harness/checklist.md` with dates and bundle paths. Leave `Reviewed` blank.

## Negative test (required)

**Prove the gate can fail, twice over.**

First, temporarily break one tier M criterion — for example move `oracle.json` aside — and confirm `lab-verify-p1`
reports that criterion FAIL, exits non-zero, and the per-tier total drops. Restore and confirm it returns to green.

Second, temporarily alter one byte of `contracts/results-row.md` in place and confirm the contract-freeze check (W010-5)
**fails**. Restore the exact original content and confirm the hash matches again. A freeze check that cannot detect a
single-byte change is not a freeze check.
