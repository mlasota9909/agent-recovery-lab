## Objective

Provide the tiered acceptance gate for P3.1, mechanically re-derive every P3.1 criterion, prove no frozen contract
changed and no earlier criterion was weakened, and record that P3 was accepted only after this remediation.

## Background (read before editing)

Follow the shape of `bin/lab-verify-p2.1`/`bin/lab-verify-p3` exactly: per-criterion checks, a tier label on each,
per-tier totals printed separately (Article XIII). Load-bearing criteria are re-derived live, not read from Z001–Z003
logs. The old→new criterion mapping (AC-P3.1-13) is the safeguard that this tranche did not buy green by weakening a
check; it must list every prior gate criterion whose mechanism changed (AC-P2-17, AC-P3-17, AC-P3-18 at minimum) and
show the asserted property is preserved or stronger.

## Steps

1. Create `bin/lab-verify-p3.1` covering AC-P3.1-01 through AC-P3.1-16. Each check prints `ID | TIER | PASS/FAIL |
   detail`; print `TIER M <p>/<n>`, `TIER A <p>/<n>` and the total on separate lines; exit non-zero on any failure.
2. Re-derive, do not trust:
   - **AC-P3.1-04**: run `bin/lab-gate-readonly-check` against every `bin/lab-verify*` live and assert evidence
     byte-identical.
   - **AC-P3.1-08 / AC-P3.1-10**: run `bin/lab-regression` and `bin/lab-verify-p3` live.
   - **AC-P3.1-11**: recompute the SC-01 headline hashes and compare to the P3 close.
   - **AC-P3.1-12**: recompute all P0/P1/P2/P2.1/P3 frozen-contract hashes and compare to recorded freezes.
3. Produce `logs/review/Z004/criterion-mapping.json`: for every prior-gate criterion whose mechanism changed in P3.1,
   record `{old_id, old_mechanism, new_mechanism, property_preserved: true, evidence}`. AC-P3.1-13 asserts each entry
   preserves or strengthens its property; the gate fails if any entry weakens one.
4. Update `docs/p3-acceptance-memo.md`: add a machine-readable block recording the two remediated defects
   (`gate_mutates_evidence`, `impossible_nested_regression`), the resolutions (`article_xvi`,
   `readonly_enforced_by_manifest`, `regression_against_fixtures`), and `p3_accepted_only_after: "P3.1"`. Assert on the
   JSON fields.
5. Confirm `bin/lab-verify`, `-p1`, `-p1.1`, `-p2`, `-p2.1`, `-p3` all green under the redesign (record totals). Note
   these now run against their fixtures / live state via `bin/lab-regression`, not against the frozen headline.
6. Update the P3.1 checklist row for Z004. Do not tick `Reviewed`.

## Negative test (required)

Two parts. **Gate can fail:** in a scratch environment, reintroduce one defect (e.g. restore the `lab-verify-p2`
copy-onto-current-evidence behaviour) and confirm `bin/lab-verify-p3.1` reports AC-P3.1-06/04 FAIL and exits non-zero.
**Weakening tripwire:** add a `criterion-mapping.json` entry that sets `property_preserved:false` (a weakened
criterion) and confirm AC-P3.1-13 goes red. Revert both and confirm green. This proves the gate catches both a
regression to a mutating gate and an attempt to buy green by weakening a check.
