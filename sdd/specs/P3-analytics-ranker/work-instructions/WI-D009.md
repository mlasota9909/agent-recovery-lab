## Objective

Prove the three things that make P3 a real result: on the SC-01 incident Arm A's recovery-point error is strictly
lower than the naive comparator's on the primary metric; stripping provenance from the ledger view leaves Arm A's
coordinate byte-identical; and no arm read the injection log or oracle optimum. This is the tranche's reason for
existing.

## Background

The headline is the whole point: evidence-driven selection should beat "roll back to roughly when I think it broke" on
a near-instant bulk deletion with concurrent legitimate work. The primary metric is **damaged rows retained** (lower
is better), tie-broken by **recovery-point LSN error**. Both numbers come straight from the D008 results rows, which
the oracle computed. Crucially: if Arm A does **not** beat naive, D009 FAILs and reports a blocker for operator
investigation of the analytics/ranker — the naive error parameter is fixed in config and is never adjusted to
manufacture a win (ADR-P3-3). The provenance-blindness test is the Article II enforcement the whole design rests on.

## Steps

1. **Headline (AC-P3-14).** Read `damaged_rows_retained` and `recovery_point_lsn_error` for `arm_a` and `naive` from
   the D008 results rows. Assert `arm_a` strictly better on the primary metric (fewer damaged rows retained; ties
   broken by smaller LSN error). Write `headline.md` with both arms' numbers side by side and the verdict. If Arm A
   does not win, set RESULT=FAIL and BLOCKERS naming the suspected cause; do not touch the naive parameter.
2. **Provenance-blindness (AC-P3-15).** Produce a provenance-stripped ledger view (drop `session_user`/`usename`/UID)
   and re-run `bin/lab-analytics` → `bin/lab-candidates` → `bin/lab-rank` against it. Assert the Arm A chosen
   coordinate is **byte-identical** to the D006 coordinate produced with the normal (already principal-free) view.
   Capture both coordinates and their `diff` (empty).
3. **No-peek (AC-P3-16).** Scan the actual inputs opened by `bin/lab-rank` (and the analytics/candidates it consumes)
   during a run — via strace/opened-file log or an injected read-tripwire — and confirm `injection_log.json` and any
   oracle-optimum artefact were never opened by the detection path. Confirm `selection_inputs` flags are all `false`.

## Negative test (required)

Two faults, each caught then reverted:
1. **Provenance leak** — modify `bin/lab-candidates` (or the ranker) to read the principal field and prefer
   `lab_agent`-touched rows; assert (a) the strip-provenance `diff` (D009-2) becomes non-empty — the coordinate moves —
   and (b) the no-peek scan flags the provenance read. This demonstrates the test genuinely catches a leak, not that a
   comment claims blindness. Revert → identical again.
2. **Peek at the answer** — point the ranker at `injection_log.json`; assert the no-peek scan (D009-3) fails and the
   D008 peek-flag rejection fires. Revert → passes.
