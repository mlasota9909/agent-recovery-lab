## Objective

Establish the constitutional principle that a frozen contract may only be changed by a superseding, separately-frozen
amendment, and record the C008 §4 deviation formally as ADR-P2.1-1 — before any code is touched. This closes the
governance class behind the defect.

## Background (read before editing)

`bin/lab-ledger-account` (C008) dropped `xid` from the frozen `capture-completeness.md` §4 mapping key and substituted
greedy file-order pairing, recording it only in an observations note. Nothing constitutional forbade that, and the P2
gate did not catch it. Article XV generalises the fix into a rule; ADR-P2.1-1 records the specific instance and its
resolution. The three prior review-driven articles are XII (no test hatches in boundary components), XIII
(verification tiers) and XIV (provenance of record); XV is the fourth.

## Steps

1. Append **Article XV** to `sdd/.specify/memory/constitution.md`, using the exact text in `plan.md` (Constitutional
   change). Do not renumber or edit any existing article. Confirm the running article count becomes 15.
2. Create `docs/adr/ADR-P2.1-1-capture-completeness-s4-deviation.md` recording:
   - the frozen clause (`capture-completeness.md` §4, DML and DDL keys, verbatim);
   - what C008 shipped instead (drop `xid`; `(table, action, natural_key)` + greedy 1:1 file order), with a pointer to
     the C008 observations note and review bundle;
   - the wal2json evidence that `xid` was absent on the parsed change records (quote the observed record shape);
   - the soundness consequence (unsound under repeated keys — the co-occurring gap+duplicate masking);
   - the resolution: recover `xid` from the transaction envelope (ADR-P2.1-2), restore the original key, withdraw the
     greedy fallback, and amend via `capture-completeness.amendment-1.md` under Article XV;
   - status `accepted`, dated.
3. Do **not** edit `capture-completeness.md`. Do **not** edit `bin/lab-ledger-account` (that is Y002). Do not freeze
   the amendment here (that is Y002) — but confirm the shipped `capture-completeness.amendment-1.md` is present and
   references this ADR.
