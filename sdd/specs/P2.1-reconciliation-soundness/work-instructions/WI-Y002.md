## Objective

Restore `xid` to the workload-to-ledger mapping key by capturing it from the wal2json transaction envelope on the DB
side and from `txid_current()` on the workload side, bring `bin/lab-ledger-account` into conformance with the amended
contract, remove the greedy file-order fallback from the primary path, and freeze `capture-completeness.amendment-1.md`.
This is the root-cause repair of the reconciliation defect.

## Background (read before editing)

wal2json format-version 2 emits transaction-envelope records: a begin `{"action":"B","xid":<int>, ...}`, then the
change records (`I`/`U`/`D`), then a commit `{"action":"C"}`. The `xid` lives on the begin record; every change up to
the commit belongs to that transaction. C008 parsed only the change records and so never saw `xid`. The fix is to
consume the envelope and stamp `xid` onto each emitted record. Independently, the workload generator can record
`txid_current()` at issue time; masked to the low 32 bits it matches wal2json's xid width, giving a provenance-free
cross-check. Read `logs/review/C008/` and the current `bin/lab-consumer` envelope handling before changing anything.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the lab is healthy first.** `bin/lab-ready` must exit 0. If not, follow the slot-recovery runbook
   (`docs/runbook-slot-recovery.md`) — never create a fresh slot. After any reset, wait on `bin/lab-barrier`.
2. **DB side — `bin/lab-consumer`.** Change the wal2json invocation and/or parser so the transaction envelope is read
   (do not disable envelope output). Capture the begin record's `xid` and stamp it as an integer `xid` field on every
   `change` and `ddl` record written to `db.jsonl`, until the matching commit. Heartbeats are exempt. Do not change any
   other field. Confirm wal2json options are stock (no bespoke parser; Article V).
3. **Workload side — `bin/lab-capture-workload`.** For each issued operation, record `xid` = `txid_current() & (2^32 - 1)`
   (low 32 bits) in the `workload.jsonl` object. Do not alter operation semantics.
4. **Mapping — `bin/lab-ledger-account`.** Change the mapping key to `(xid, table, action, natural_key)` (DML) and
   `(xid, command_tag, object_identity)` (DDL), as a bijection over the ledgerable set. **Remove the greedy
   file-order pairing from the primary path.** The mapping must not read `principal`.
5. **Freeze the amendment.** Finalise `sdd/specs/P2.1-reconciliation-soundness/contracts/capture-completeness.amendment-1.md`
   to match what you implemented (it already prescribes the envelope path). Record its SHA-256 in `logs/review/Y002/`
   and mark it frozen. Do **not** edit `capture-completeness.md`.
6. **Re-verify no regression.** Re-run the C007 unique-key acceptance workload through capture + reconciliation and
   confirm ops=500, mapped=500, gaps=0, duplicates=0 under the new key. Confirm `bin/lab-verify-p2` still 18/18.
7. If — and only if — you can show with captured evidence that the envelope `xid` is genuinely unrecoverable, stop and
   report BLOCKED with the evidence; do not silently switch to the `op_uid` fallback (ADR-P2.1-2). The fallback needs a
   `capture-completeness.amendment-2.md` and operator sign-off.

## Negative test (required)

Two parts.

**`xid`-drop probe.** In a scratch copy of `bin/lab-consumer`, revert to emitting change records **without** the
envelope `xid` (the C008 behaviour), capture a short incident, and run reconciliation on a **repeated-key** micro-workload
(two updates of one natural key). Assert the mapping either cannot key (missing `xid`) or falls back to ambiguous
pairing — i.e. Y002's key-based checks FAIL. This proves the `xid` is actually load-bearing, not decorative. Restore
the corrected consumer and confirm the checks pass.

**Provenance-leak probe.** Temporarily make the mapping read `principal` in a scratch copy; run Y002-6; assert the
provenance-strip test now changes the result (i.e. the leak is detectable). Restore and confirm Y002-6 passes.
