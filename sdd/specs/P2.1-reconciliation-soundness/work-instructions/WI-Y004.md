## Objective

Provide the tiered acceptance gate for P2.1, mechanically re-derive every P2.1 criterion, prove the P0/P1/P2 frozen
contracts (including `capture-completeness.md`) are unchanged, and update the acceptance memo to record that P2 was
accepted only after this remediation.

## Background (read before editing)

Follow the shape of `bin/lab-verify-p2` exactly: per-criterion checks, a tier label on each, and per-tier totals
printed separately (Article XIII). No criterion may be judged by searching prose. Where a P2.1 criterion was proven
by an earlier task, this gate **re-derives** it from the committed artefacts rather than trusting that task's log —
for AC-P2.1-05/06/09/10/11 that means actually invoking capture/reconciliation, not reading Y002/Y003 JSON.

## Steps

1. Create `bin/lab-verify-p2.1` covering AC-P2.1-01 through AC-P2.1-16. Each check prints `ID | TIER | PASS/FAIL |
   detail`. Compute and print `TIER M <p>/<n>`, `TIER A <p>/<n>`, and the total on separate lines. Exit non-zero if any
   check fails.
2. Re-derive, do not trust:
   - **AC-P2.1-02 / AC-P2.1-15**: recompute SHA-256 of `capture-completeness.md`, the P0 (R007), P1 (W002/W010) and P2
     (C001) frozen contracts, and compare to their recorded freezes.
   - **AC-P2.1-05 / AC-P2.1-06**: capture a short incident live and re-derive the `xid` presence and cross-check.
   - **AC-P2.1-09 / AC-P2.1-10 / AC-P2.1-11**: run reconciliation live on the C007 workload and on the repeated-key
     workload (clean), and on an injected-fault copy (must be detected).
   - **AC-P2.1-13**: invoke `bin/lab-verify-p2` and assert 18/18, exit 0.
3. Update `docs/p2-acceptance-memo.md`: add a machine-readable block recording the remediated defect
   (`reconciliation_key_deviation`), the resolution (`xid_recovered_from_envelope`), the amendment reference
   (`capture-completeness.amendment-1.md`), and `p2_accepted_only_after: "P2.1"`. Keep prose to a minimum; assert on
   the JSON fields.
4. Confirm `bin/lab-verify`, `bin/lab-verify-p1`, `bin/lab-verify-p1.1` all still green (regression guard). Record their
   totals in the bundle.
5. Update the P2.1 checklist row for Y004. Do not tick `Reviewed`.

## Negative test (required)

Two parts. **Gate can fail:** in a scratch environment, corrupt one re-derived input (e.g. point the gate at a
`db.jsonl` with a null `xid`, or a reconciliation with an injected gap) and confirm `bin/lab-verify-p2.1` reports that
specific criterion FAIL and exits non-zero. **Frozen-contract tripwire:** append a single byte to a scratch copy of
`capture-completeness.md`, run the hash re-verify, and confirm AC-P2.1-02/15 go red. Restore and confirm green. This
proves the gate would catch both a soundness regression and an illicit edit of the frozen contract.
