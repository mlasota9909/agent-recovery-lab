# WI-C005 — DB stream integrity: before-images on U/D + DDL in one LSN-ordered stream

## Objective

Prove the decoded DB stream in `db.jsonl` carries the two things the whole experiment depends on: a full before-image
on every UPDATE and DELETE, and every DDL operation — all in one non-decreasing-LSN stream.

## Background

This is the highest-consequence silent-failure task in the tranche. If `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL` regresses on a
relation, UPDATE/DELETE records lose their old tuple and **damaged-data retention can no longer be scored** — and
nothing else would notice until the analysis. Separately, logical decoding does not surface DDL on its own; the
`lab_ddl_audit` event trigger (installed by `bin/lab-schema-init`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, in the publication) is what
puts `CREATE`/`ALTER`/`DROP` into the same stream in LSN order. SC-03 depends on that.

## Steps

1. Build/complete a verifier `bin/lab-db-integrity <incident>` that reads `db.jsonl` and asserts, against the
   capture-completeness contract §2:
   - every `action:"U"` and `action:"D"` change record has a non-empty `identity` (full old tuple), not a key-only
     identity;
   - every DDL operation issued appears as a `kind:"ddl"` record with the right `command_tag` and `object_identity`;
   - records are in non-decreasing integer-LSN order (`(high<<32)+low`).
2. Confirm the scenario relations have `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL` set (query `pg_class.relreplident`); record the
   observed value per relation.
3. Do **not** re-implement the consumer or the DDL trigger; verify the existing ones produce conformant records.

## Acceptance tests

| ID | Tier | Command | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C005-1 | M | insert a row, update it, delete it; read the U and D records from `db.jsonl` | each carries `identity` equal to the correct pre-change tuple (AC-P2-08) |
| C005-2 | M | `ALTER TABLE` and `DROP TABLE` a scenario relation; read `db.jsonl` | both appear as `ddl` records with correct `command_tag`/`object_identity`, in LSN order (AC-P2-09) |
| C005-3 | M | `bin/lab-db-integrity <inc>` over the window | exits 0; monotonic LSN, before-images present, DDL present |
| C005-4 | M | query `relreplident` for each scenario relation | all show FULL (`f`) |

## Negative test (required)

Set `REPLICA IDENTITY DEFAULT` on one scenario relation, perform a DELETE, and assert the decoded record's `identity`
now carries **only the key**, so `bin/lab-db-integrity` fails the before-image check. Capture both the degraded record
and the FULL record side by side. Restore `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL`, repeat, and assert the check passes. **Keep the
side-by-side excerpt** — it is methodology evidence that the capture design is sound rather than assumed. A
verifier that passes with key-only identities is a fail.

## Review bundle

Standard five, plus `before-image-ud.json`, `ddl-records.json`, `replica-identity.log`, and the FULL-vs-DEFAULT
side-by-side excerpt.

## Operator review checklist

- [ ] All scenario relations are `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL`
- [ ] The `DEFAULT`-regression negative test lost the before-image, then recovered it
