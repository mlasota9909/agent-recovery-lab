## Objective

Re-scope the P5 parity property to the `model_view` surface, relabel the F009 headline to the recovery probe PB-6,
re-derive `bin/lab-verify-p5` to an honest green under those two changes, and prove the R0/R1/R2 representation
artefacts are byte-identical to their P5 close (P5.1 changed the interface surface, not the representations).

## Background (read before editing)

P5's parity criterion asserted "only `content`+`telemetry` differ across R0/R1/R2" on the full response envelope. Now
that the subject sees only the `model_view`, parity must be asserted on the `model_view` surface — a strengthening, not
a weakening (the answer is no longer part of what is compared, but it was identical anyway). The F009 headline was
computed on PB-4; the recovery probe is PB-6. R2 evidence-bytes are strictly and materially < R0 on PB-6 (the numbers
already exist in the F009 artefacts — this is a relabel, not a re-run). Any criterion mechanism/label change is recorded
for G004's old→new mapping; the frozen contract files stay byte-identical.

## Steps

1. Re-scope the parity check: for each of the six probes, emit the `model_view` for R0/R1/R2 via
   `bin/lab-repr-modelview` and assert the envelopes are structurally identical (same keys, same `incident_id`,
   `probe_id`, `params`), differing only in `content` and `telemetry`. Re-derive from live emission, not from a stored
   claim. (AC-P5.1-09)
2. Relabel the headline: from the committed F009 artefacts, read the R0 and R2 `evidence_bytes` for
   `PB-6-candidate-windows`; assert R2 strictly and materially less than R0 (define "materially" as the same threshold
   P5 used — e.g. R2 <= 25% of R0; use P5's own definition, do not invent a looser one). Record the PB-6 delta as the
   reported headline number. (AC-P5.1-10)
3. Prove no representation was rebuilt: re-verify the F002 (R0), F003 (R1), F004 (R2) artefact hashes against their
   recorded values in `logs/review/`. All byte-identical. (AC-P5.1-11)
4. Provenance-blindness on the subject surface: strip-scan every `model_view` and its `content` (and any R2 summary
   surfaced) for `principal`/`usename`/`session_user`/UID; none present. (AC-P5.1-12)
5. Re-derive `bin/lab-verify-p5`: its parity criterion now asserts on the `model_view` surface (step 1); its headline
   criterion now asserts on PB-6 (step 2). Every other P5 criterion unchanged. The gate reaches honest green with
   per-tier totals. Run it under `bin/lab-gate-readonly-check` and confirm evidence byte-identical. (AC-P5.1-14)
6. Record the two criterion changes (parity surface; headline probe) for G004's old→new mapping, with the property each
   still asserts. Commit before gating. Update the checklist row for G003.

## Negative test (required)

Prove the parity check catches a real interface divergence: temporarily make `bin/lab-repr-modelview` add a stray key
(e.g. `note`) to R2's model_view only, re-run G003-1, and confirm the parity check fails naming the divergent key;
revert and confirm it passes. Separately, confirm the headline check would fail if pointed at a probe where R2 is not
materially smaller (point it at a synthetic equal-bytes fixture in scratch) — proving it is not a rubber stamp. Revert.
