## Objective

Record the principle and the design before any change lands: append **Article XVII** to the constitution, and write
the three ADRs that record the confound, the `model_view`/`reference` split, and the guard mechanism. This task writes
no code and touches no representation.

## Background (read before editing)

The P5 review found that the frozen `representation-interface` response envelope co-locates `content` (evidence the
model reads) with `answer` (the pre-computed result, which for `PB-6-candidate-windows` is the recovery coordinate).
Nothing stops P6 from handing the model the `answer`, which would void the R0/R1/R2 ablation. This is the sixth
review-driven class, after Articles XII (test hatches), XIII (verification tiers), XIV (provenance of record), XV
(amendment by supersession) and XVI (read-only gates). Read `sdd/.specify/memory/constitution.md` first and confirm
sixteen articles are in force; G001 adds the seventeenth.

## Steps

1. Append Article XVII to `sdd/.specify/memory/constitution.md`, verbatim from `plan.md` §"Constitutional change".
   Do not renumber or edit any existing article. Confirm the article count becomes 17.
2. Create `docs/adr/ADR-P5.1-1-answer-key-in-subject-view.md` recording:
   - D1 with evidence: quote `representation-interface` §4 (envelope has `answer`+`content`), hard rule R3 (`answer`
     identical across R0/R1/R2), and §5 (PB-6 designated the recovery probe). State why P6 passing the envelope voids
     the ablation, and that `answer` has no P6 scoring role (P6 scores via the P4 restore+validate path).
   - D2: the F009 headline was labelled PB-4; the recovery probe is PB-6; R2<R0 on both so the result holds but the
     reported probe must be PB-6.
3. Create `docs/adr/ADR-P5.1-2-modelview-reference-split.md`: the split defined in the amendment, and the decision to
   preserve (not delete) `answer` as a separate `reference` used only by parity/telemetry.
4. Create `docs/adr/ADR-P5.1-3-modelview-guard.md`: the guard mechanism — what it forbids (structured `answer`,
   coordinate-answer fields, provenance tokens) and what it permits (LSN digits inside `content`), and that this makes
   Article XVII tier-M enforceable.
5. Update the checklist row for G001.
