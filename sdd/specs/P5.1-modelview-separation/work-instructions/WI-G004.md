## Objective

Build the P5.1 gate that re-derives every P5.1 criterion mechanically with per-tier totals; re-verify every frozen
contract hash is unchanged; record the old→new criterion mapping proving nothing was weakened; and update the
acceptance memo to record that P5 was accepted only after P5.1.

## Background (read before editing)

This gate is the tranche's own acceptance and must itself obey Article XVI (read-only over evidence) and Article XVII
(it may construct adversarial model_views only in `logs/review/<id>/scratch/`, never in the evidence tree). The old→new
mapping is the safeguard against buying green by weakening: for every P5 criterion whose mechanism or label changed
(parity surface; headline probe), the mapping states the original property and shows it is preserved or strengthened.

## Steps

1. Build `bin/lab-verify-p5.1` running every AC-P5.1-01..16 check, each tier-labelled, per-tier totals printed
   separately (no single undifferentiated total). It re-derives, not reads narrative:
   - re-runs `bin/lab-modelview-guard` across R0/R1/R2 × 6 probes (all pass);
   - re-checks the guard refuses an injected answer and an injected provenance token (constructed in scratch);
   - re-verifies the amendment freeze and the base contract byte-identical;
   - re-verifies F002/F003/F004 artefact hashes and all prior frozen contract hashes;
   - re-runs the model_view parity and the PB-6 headline assertions;
   - runs `bin/lab-gate-readonly-check` against `bin/lab-verify-p5` and against itself.
2. Build `logs/review/G004/criterion-mapping.json`: every P5 criterion → its P5.1 status (`unchanged` /
   `mechanism-changed` / `label-changed`), the property asserted, and for changed ones a `strictness: preserved|strengthened`
   flag with a one-line justification. Assert no entry is `weakened`. (AC-P5.1-13)
3. Re-verify all prior frozen contract hashes (P0/P1/P2/P2.1/P3/P4/P5 incl. `representation-interface.md`,
   `representation-fidelity.md`, `capture-completeness.md`) unchanged. (AC-P5.1-13)
4. Update `docs/p5-acceptance-memo.md` with machine-readable fields: `remediated_defect: "answer-key-in-subject-view"`,
   `headline_probe: "PB-6-candidate-windows"`, `p5_accepted_only_after: "P5.1"`. (AC-P5.1-16)
5. Run `bin/lab-verify-p5.1`; confirm green with per-tier totals; run it under `bin/lab-gate-readonly-check`. Commit.
   Update the checklist row for G004.

## Negative test (required)

Prove the mapping tripwire is real: add a scratch copy of the mapping with one criterion marked `weakened` (or with a
changed criterion whose property no longer holds) and confirm `bin/lab-verify-p5.1` exits non-zero on G004-2; revert
and confirm green. This shows the gate cannot pass while any criterion is weakened.
