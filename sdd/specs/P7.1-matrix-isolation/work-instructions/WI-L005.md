## Objective

Replace the conflated SC-04 headline with an honest, disaggregated one that separates the actionability dimension from
the compactness dimension, states SC-04's compactness counter-result plainly, scopes the compactness claim to where it
holds, and keeps SC-01 as the sole P4-validated headline. This closes D3. Runs in both branches: under FULL it consumes
the L004 validated rows; under FALLBACK it consumes the P7 rows and states D2/D3 as limitations.

## Background (read before editing)

`docs/P7-handoff.json` sold SC-04 as `strongest_figure: true` with `matches_prediction: false`, `evidence_bytes_r2:
1099` > `evidence_bytes_r0: 799`, `token_r2: 302` > `token_r0: 198`. Per ADR-P7.1-5 these are two claims:
**actionability** (R2 0.95 vs R0 0.0 — the largest arm-quality gap in the matrix, a genuine strong result) and
**compactness** (on a tiny 799-byte incident R2's overhead made it larger — the universal-compactness claim fails at
small scale). No number changes; the mislabel is removed and each claim is scoped to its truth. SC-01 remains the only
P4-validated headline (R2 5/5, R0 1/5, R1 0/5, all safe-but-conservative).

## Steps

1. Build `bin/lab-headline` (or extend the existing headline emitter) to write `logs/review/L005/headline.json` with,
   per scenario, **separate** fields: `actionability:{determination_rate_r0/r1/r2, verdict, quality_r0/r1/r2}` and
   `compactness:{evidence_bytes_r0/r1/r2, tokens_r0/r1/r2, compactness_holds:bool}`. Replace the scalar
   `strongest_figure` with `strongest_actionability_gap:<scenario>` (computed, not asserted) and a per-scenario
   `compactness_holds`.
2. Compute the SC-04 actionability gap (`quality_r2 - quality_r0`) from committed rows and assert it equals the largest
   arm-quality gap in the matrix; set SC-04 `compactness_holds:false` with `evidence_bytes_r2 > evidence_bytes_r0`
   shown. Set SC-01 `compactness_holds:true` (the ~140x case) as the scope where compactness holds.
3. Mark SC-01 as `p4_validated:true` and the sole validated headline; under FULL, mark SC-02 `p4_validated:true` and
   SC-03/04 `set_validated:true`; under FALLBACK, mark SC-02/03/04 `preliminary:true` with the D2 limitation string.
4. Write `headline.md`: one paragraph stating the actionability finding (representation drives whether a
   modest model can act — holds across scales, SC-04 strongest), one stating the compactness finding (a scale-dependent
   bonus — large on SC-01, inverts on tiny SC-04), and the SC-01 validated result.
5. Verify the SC-01 banked headline is byte-identical: re-hash `db.jsonl` and `results/SC-01.jsonl` against the P6.1
   recorded hashes.
6. Commit. Update the checklist row for L005.
