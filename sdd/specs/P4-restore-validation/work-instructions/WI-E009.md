## Objective

Prove the two things that make P4 a real result and certify the whole measurement apparatus: restoring the **known
ground-truth coordinate** yields a `correct` verdict with zero mismatches; and validation **discriminates** — a
post-damage coordinate does not validate clean, and a too-early coordinate reports legitimate loss. This is the
tranche's reason for existing and the walking-skeleton's closing test.

## Background

If feeding the perfect coordinate does not reproduce the pre-incident state exactly, the harness is wrong and every
P3/P6 number is meaningless — so this is the test that certifies the apparatus, not just this tranche. The ground-truth
coordinate is the known optimum for the SC-01 incident (the latest safe point before the malicious commit); it is
labelled `restore_of=ground_truth` and is exempt from the no-peek check by definition (ADR-P4-6). Discrimination is
what proves a `correct` verdict means something: a validator that always says `correct` is worthless.

## Steps

1. **Headline (AC-P4-13).** Obtain the SC-01 ground-truth coordinate (the oracle's known optimum). Run
   `lab-restore` -> `lab-validate` -> `lab-verdict` with `restore_of=ground_truth`. Assert
   `consistency_verdict=correct` and `summary` all zero (`damage_rows_retained=0`, `legit_rows_lost=0`,
   `files_mismatched=0`, `schema_diffs=0`), all read from `report.json`. If not clean, set RESULT=FAIL and BLOCKERS
   naming the suspected layer (replay / clone / validation) — do not adjust anything to force clean.
2. **Discrimination — too late (AC-P4-14).** Restore a coordinate strictly **after** the malicious commit; assert the
   verdict is `incorrect-corrupt` with `damage_rows_retained>0`.
3. **Discrimination — too early (AC-P4-14).** Restore a coordinate strictly **before** some legitimate concurrent
   work; assert the verdict is `incorrect-safe` with `legit_rows_lost>0` and `damage_rows_retained=0`.
4. Write `headline.md` with the three cases side by side (ground-truth / too-late / too-early), their summaries and
   verdicts. Tear down all sandbox resources; confirm victim+evidence manifest unchanged.

## Negative test (required)

Make validation ignore file-level mismatches (drop the byte-hash comparison) and confirm the ground-truth case still
passes but the **too-late** case is now wrongly reported `correct` — i.e. discrimination breaks — proving E009-2
genuinely depends on real comparison, not a constant. Revert -> too-late is `incorrect-corrupt` again. (This is the
"validation is a rubber stamp" failure mode, caught.)
