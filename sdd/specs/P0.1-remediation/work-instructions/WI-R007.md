## Objective

Close the tranche. Re-run everything, confirm the five findings are actually closed, update the P0 sign-off artefacts
to tell the truth about what happened, and write the memo that lets P1 start.

## Why the memo matters beyond housekeeping

Two audiences. **You**, when you need to state in a few weeks how the substrate was verified without
reconstructing it from memory. And **the reviewer**, who will be more convinced by a build that was reviewed,
found defective in three specific ways, and repaired with evidence, than by a build reported as passing everything at
the first attempt. Record the remediation as part of the method, not as an embarrassment to hide.

## Steps

1. Confirm every prior task in this tranche has a review bundle whose acceptance rows are all PASS and tier-labelled.
   If any is missing or red, stop — this task does not paper over an incomplete tranche.
2. Run the full suite: `bin/lab-verify` and `bin/lab-verify --json`. Capture both. Confirm per-tier totals print and no
   single undifferentiated headline appears.
3. Run each remediation-specific check once more, in one sitting, and capture the results together:
   - `bin/lab-isolation-test` (all assertions)
   - `bin/lab-isolation-test --enumerate`
   - `bin/lab-isolation-test --regression`
   - `bin/lab-slot --check` with the consumer running (expect 0)
   - `bin/lab-consumer status` for an open incident
   - `bin/lab-reset-guard-test`
   - `bin/lab-environment`
4. **Findings closure table.** For each of F-1 through F-5, state: the finding, the repair, the acceptance criterion
   that proves it, the observed evidence, and CLOSED or OPEN. Any OPEN finding must name what remains and why.
5. **Idempotence sweep.** Re-run the full suite a second time and confirm identical criterion results. A suite whose
   results drift between runs cannot support a reproducibility claim.
6. Update `sdd/specs/P0-lab-substrate/checklist.md`:
   - append a dated note to each affected task row indicating remediation by P0.1;
   - update the tranche-level gates that R002 through R006 now discharge;
   - add to the *Known limitations* list: that P0 was accepted only after remediation; that
     acceptance criteria are reported in two tiers with the load-bearing ones mechanical; and that the evidence
     channel's accepted command surface is enumerated and tested.
7. Write `docs/p0-acceptance-memo.md`: what P0 delivers, the five findings and their closure, the tier split with
   counts, the measured facts worth citing (steady-state retained WAL, full-cycle timing from T015, observed
   cross-layer skew from T011), the standing limitations, and an explicit statement that P0 is accepted and P1 may
   begin.
8. Record the measured full-cycle time from T015 and state plainly what it implies for the P1 stratified sample-size
   plan. This is the number that replaces "maximum achievable sample will be attempted" — an open-ended target with
   no plan behind it.

## Negative test (required)

**N-1, the gate can refuse.** Temporarily break one criterion — the simplest safe choice is to stop the consumer, which
should trip `bin/lab-slot --check` — and confirm the full suite exits non-zero and names the failing criterion with its
tier. Restore and confirm green.

A gate task that cannot fail is the same defect this whole tranche exists to fix, so this test is not optional.

## Non-regression (required)

- All eighteen original P0 criteria still pass, with substance unchanged. Reference R004's before/after table.
- No contract under `specs/P0-lab-substrate/contracts/` was modified: prove with a diff against the frozen versions.
- The lab is left in a usable state for P1: consumer running or cleanly stopped, no open incident half-captured, no
  temporary key material, no neutralised guard, no scratch files under `EVIDENCE_ROOT`.
