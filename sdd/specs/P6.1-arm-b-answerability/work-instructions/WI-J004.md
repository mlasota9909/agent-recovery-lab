## Objective

Re-run SC-01 Arm B across R0/R1/R2 × K on the corrected containment check and the amended grammar, and produce the
honest R0-vs-R1-vs-R2 headline the experiment turns on. Report determinations and refusals faithfully; diagnose any
residual refusal with the J001 taxonomy rather than hiding it. Retain the prior null as superseded.

## Background (read before editing)

`bin/lab-arm-b` (H005) is the only Arm B caller of `bin/lab-model`; it uses `bin/lab-arm-b-prompt` (rebuilt in J003) and
`bin/lab-arm-b-extract` (rebuilt in J003), and the containment check (rewritten in J002). Do not change the model
(model-runtime R1/R3/R4). Determinations are scored through the **unchanged** P4 restore+validate path against the
SC-01 oracle, exactly as Arm A is scored, so the arms stay comparable. Ground-truth LSN for SC-01 is `1/E1000000`
(from H009). K is the replicate count fixed in `arm-b-protocol` R8 (default 5).

## Steps

1. Quarantine the prior null: copy `logs/review/H009/headline.json` to
   `logs/review/H009/headline.superseded.json` (retain, do not delete) and add a pointer note to the J004 re-run
   location. (AC-P6.1-16)
2. For each representation R0/R1/R2 and each replicate 0..K-1: assemble the guarded prompt, verify containment
   (`containment_verified:true`), invoke the pinned model, capture the verbatim completion, real `prompt_tokens` /
   `completion_tokens` / `latency_ms`, extract the outcome, and — for a determination — resolve the coordinate and score
   it through the P4 path (verdict, `damage_rows_retained`, `legit_rows_lost`, coordinate error vs `1/E1000000`).
   Record every run to the arm-b replicate schema; retain all completions.
3. Emit `logs/review/J004/headline.json` with, per representation: `determinations`, `refusals`, `refusal_rate`,
   `evidence_bytes`, `prompt_tokens_real`; and for determinations, the P4 verdict distribution, median
   `damage_rows_retained`, median `legit_rows_lost`, and coordinate error. Include replicate agreement (identical-
   coordinate rate) per representation (R8).
4. For any residual refusal, attach its J001-taxonomy cause (re-run `bin/lab-diagnose-refusal` on the new completions).
   If a representation still refuses at 100% under the corrected instrument, state it plainly in
   `headline.md` as a candidate genuine finding (the modest model is insufficient for SC-01 from that representation) —
   do not hide it, do not coerce a determination.
5. Harness determinism (R7): pick one recorded completion, re-run extraction + scoring over it, and confirm a
   byte-identical coordinate and score (AC-P6.1-14).
6. Provenance sweep across all new completions/rationales: no `principal`/`usename`/`session_user`/UID (Article II).
7. Commit. Update the checklist row for J004.

## Negative test (required)

Take one recorded **determination** completion and corrupt its tagged coordinate line to an out-of-range LSN in a
scratch copy; re-run extraction and confirm the outcome flips to `refusal` (`lsn_int=null`), **not** a clamped or
guessed coordinate. Revert and confirm the determination and its score return byte-identical. This proves scoring
cannot be salvaged from a bad locator into a false determination. (AC-P6.1-14)
